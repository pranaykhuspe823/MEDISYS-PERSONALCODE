Hospital Settings

Manage how your hospital's portal looks for your staff and patients.

Hospital Logo

Once set, this replaces the CORE5 MEDISYS logo in the header for your hospital only — every staff member's portal and every patient your hospital registers will see it. Other hospitals are never affected.

No custom logo set — using the default CORE5 MEDISYS logo.

Recommended: a square logo (1:1, e.g. 512×512px) or a landscape logo close to 2:1 (e.g. 700×350px, the same shape as the default logo) — PNG with a transparent background works best. Max 5MB.

Footer Branding

Once set, this replaces "CORE5 MEDISYS" in the footer with your own hospital name — for your hospital only, everywhere your staff and patients see it. The "Powered by CORE5 MEDISYS" credit stays and can't be changed.
